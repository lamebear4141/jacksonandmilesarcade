Visiting
One high-five per friend per day — make it count!
The entire social surface is five buttons. No comments, no text, no downvotes — reactions are a fixed positive set, and the high-five is once per friend per day (enforced by date-keyed docs, so it's idempotent by construction). The visitor gives +5🪙 to the owner and gets +2🪙 back — generosity pays both ways, so checking on friends' worlds becomes a daily ritual. Who can visit: family always; approved friends only via the parent-approval flow from the Friends page. Worlds are never listed publicly and never ranked against each other.

🧱 + ⚡ → 🏗️ build your world → 👥 friends visit → 🖐️ high-fives → 🪙 coins → 🎨 avatar gear & rare decorations → a cooler world → more visits → …
You can't do just one thing and grow. Work + play + sharing — that's the whole game.
Where XP fits: levels stay the universal ladder — all three pillars feed it, and levels gate land expansions, costume unlocks, and Scholar-tier items. The old "4× for practice" coin weighting retires: steering now comes from which resource a game pays, which is stronger and fairer — a third each, exactly as intended.
